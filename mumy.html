Our story

Every clutch is signed by the hand that stitched it.

Maison Verso began at a single embroidery table, reworking discarded denim into something worth keeping. Today the atelier is still small on purpose — every panel is hand-painted with floral motifs, every seam top-stitched in brass-toned thread, and every clasp set by hand before a bag ever leaves the workshop.

We believe a good object should show its making. That's why we leave the stitching visible, the fabric a little imperfect, and the story on a little brass plate stamped into every piece.
