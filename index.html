Our story

Every envelope is signed by the hand that painted it.

Maison Verso began at a single worktable, hand-painting motifs onto sheets of thick handmade paper because the shagun envelopes we could find never matched the gift going inside them. Today the studio is still small on purpose — every design is hand-painted or hand-foiled, every fold is scored and creased by hand, and every envelope is checked before it ever leaves the workshop.

Every piece is finished the same way: a delicate gold-tone chain loop and a small dangling charm, so the envelope holds the gift as beautifully as it's given.

We believe a good gift deserves a good envelope. That's why we keep the brushwork visible, the foil a little imperfect, and a small maker's mark stamped inside the flap of every piece.
